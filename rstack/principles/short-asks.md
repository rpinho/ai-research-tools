# Short asks to the human

**The rule.** What reaches the person is short, and it is a question they can answer. Not a status
report, not a review wall, not three options with trade-offs when one is obviously right. Long
documents go where they actually read them, which is probably not the terminal you are typing in.

**Enforced by** a guard that refuses to open a document on the machine and points at the review
queue instead, and by a notification script with explicit kinds, where only some of them wake
anyone. Whether a wake happens is a property of the channel, not a judgement the agent makes each
time.

The cost of getting this wrong is asymmetric. An unnecessary wake is expensive and visible; a
status update nobody needed just trains them to stop reading.
